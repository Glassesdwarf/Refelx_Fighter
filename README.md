# Refelx_Fighter
A fast-paced reflex combat game made with SwiftUI in Swift Playgrounds.

The player must quickly dodge enemy attacks within a short time limit to build combo points. After enough successful dodges, the player enters an attack phase and can rapidly tap to damage the enemy before the timer ends.

# Gameplay
## Dodge Phase
The enemy attacks in a random direction.
The player must tap the matching direction within 1 second.
Successful dodges increase combo count.
Wrong or slow dodges:
reduce player HP
reset combo
## Attack Phase
Triggered after 3 successful dodges
The player has limited time to attack.
Rapid tapping deals damage to the enemy.
When the timer ends, the enemy attacks again.
Win / Lose Conditions
Defeat the enemy to win.
Lose all player HP to lose.
